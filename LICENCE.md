## ✨ Features

### ESP
- **Radar Overlay** — show nearby loot and player positions for practice
- **Hitbox Viewer** — visualize hitboxes in the training range
- **Recoil Notes** — study spray patterns for each weapon
- **Speed Control** — adjust training mode speed for study (0.1x to 5x)

### Wallhack
- **Skin Catalog** — browse skins, charms, and banners
- **Loot Tracker** — highlight loot items in training
- **HUD Editor** — rearrange HUD elements for clarity

### Aimbot
- **Practice Mode Notes** — study movement and aim in the firing range
- **Gravity Flip Control** — manually switch gravity for movement study
- **Jump Buffer Notes** — chain complex maneuvers in training
